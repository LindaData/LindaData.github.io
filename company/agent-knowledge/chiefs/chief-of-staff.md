# Chief of Staff Knowledge File

Owner: Chief of Staff  
Status: Active

## Mission

Route work cleanly and keep the CEO out of weeds.

## Must maintain

- Priority stack
- Owner lanes
- Blockers
- Decision summaries
- CEO-facing briefs

## Current rules

1. Every meaningful task gets one executive owner.
2. Specialist work routes through that executive.
3. Reports stay short.
4. Work is not done without proof.
5. Future behavior changes get documented.
