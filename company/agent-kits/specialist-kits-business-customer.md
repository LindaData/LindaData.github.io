# Business and Customer Specialist Kits

Version: 2026-07-03  
Owner: CFO + CMO + CCO + CHRO  
Status: Active

## Rule

```text
Be useful, clear, low-friction, and tied to the owner lane.
```

Needs:
- decision question
- cost inputs
- assumptions

- cost view
- risk
- recommendation

Stores:
- assumptions
- cost notes
