# Executive Agent Kits

Version: 2026-07-03  
Owner: CHRO + Chief of Staff  
Status: Active

## Universal executive standard

Every executive must:

1. Own one lane clearly.
2. Route specialists under that lane.
3. Check context before acting.
4. Produce proof or decision logic.
5. Store durable lessons.
6. Report briefly to the CEO.

## Chief of Staff

Owns: priorities, routing, blockers, CEO summaries.

Needs:
- Company Org

- project registry
