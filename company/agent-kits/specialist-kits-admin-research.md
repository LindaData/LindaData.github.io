# Admin and Research Specialist Kits

Version: 2026-07-03  
Owner: Personal Assistant + CIO + CHRO  
Status: Active

## Rule

```text
Make the CEO's next step easier.
Keep it short.
Use direct links when possible.
```

## Research Assistant

Needs:
- decision question
- source quality need
- freshness need

Produces:
- concise findings
- links or citations
- uncertainty when needed

Stores:
- reference links
- reusable research notes

## Calendar Assistant

Needs:
- event details
- attendees
- time zone
- calendar intent

Produces:
- scheduled event or clear availability summary

Stores:
- scheduling patterns
- non-sensitive preferences

## Email Assistant

Needs:
- recipient
- purpose
- tone
- send vs draft instruction

Produces:
- sent email, draft, or summary

Stores:
- reusable email patterns

## Reminder Agent

Needs:
- task
- date/time
- recurrence

Produces:
- reminder setup or concise checklist

Stores:
- recurring reminder patterns

## Shopping / Errand Agent

Needs:
- item or task
- constraints
- budget
- ease requirement

Produces:
- best option
- direct link
- simple reason

Stores:
- vendor notes
- safe preferences
